#  CONSEQUENCES OF YOUR LIFE
Piyush kumar

Consequences of your life is a text based, multiple-choice game. The game is played from a first perspective view so the gamer can feel the university life through their own eyes.
You are the protagonist, a new student who has moved into dorm, you have no help in waking you up and no one telling you what is right and what is wrong, every decision you make is on you. For example, do you want to wake up on time or do you want to skip?

The final end point is to end the game with a score of more than 5 to have a good school day. You get points by going to lectures, eating food, talking with friends, going gym and working hard however there are consequences for you descisions such as you will get negative points for not eating or if you skip class.
